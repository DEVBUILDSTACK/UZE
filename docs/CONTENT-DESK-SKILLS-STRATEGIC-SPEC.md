## Skill 1 — growth-analytics

**Strategic purpose.** Turns every content and outreach action into a
measured decision. The Desk does not run on taste; it runs on funnel
evidence — which vertical, sequence, step, and template moved the
number, and by how much.

**USP contribution.** "Every content dollar traceable to revenue."
Competitors sell content volume; the Desk sells attributable growth.
The analytics skill is what lets us promise — and prove — ROI per
campaign, per tenant.

**CTA mechanics.** The skill doesn't just report; it optimizes the
CTA itself — which call-to-action copy, placement, and step in the
sequence converts per vertical. Findings feed directly back into
template and sequence design (closed loop).

**Tenant application.** 456iP brands: internal proving ground
(L2Launch funnel analytics first). Affiliate tenants: white-labeled
growth reports as a retention product — the affiliate *sees* what
their content earned.

**Proof / metric.** Funnel conversion deltas with significance (no
reporting on n=12); attribution per vertical per quarter.

**For the expert.** Is "attributable growth" the sharpest USP edge, or
should the promise be framed as speed, cost, or certainty? Where in the
funnel does the CTA lose most force?

## Skill 2 — x402-payments

**Strategic purpose.** Monetizes the Desk's premium surfaces
per-request, in USDC, without accounts or subscriptions — research
briefs, vertical reports, API access, data products.

**USP contribution.** "Pay per answer, not per seat." The agentic
payment rail is itself a differentiator: machine-readable pricing,
instant settlement, no procurement friction. This is the RCS engine's
cash register.

**CTA mechanics.** The CTA *is* the payment: "unlock this brief for
0.50 USDC" — one click, no signup. Metering per tenant means each
affiliate's premium content is separately monetizable.

**Tenant application.** 456iP: paid research products first. Affiliates:
their own paywalled expertise content, settled to their attribution.

**Proof / metric.** Payment-completion rate per priced surface;
revenue per tenant per month.

**For the expert.** What price points maximize unlock rate vs. revenue?
Should the first premium surface be free-to-paid graduated?

## Skill 3 — solana-anchor

**Strategic purpose.** Solana-native execution for UZE on-chain actions:
ticketing, provenance anchoring, referral settlement. Rust/Anchor
programs + TypeScript clients.

**USP contribution.** "Web3 that feels like Web2." Solana speed and
cost make on-chain ticketing and settlement invisible to the end user —
the blockchain is the plumbing, never the pitch.

**CTA mechanics.** On-chain CTAs: mint the ticket, claim the referral,
settle the resale — each a one-tap transaction inside the flow the
content created.

**Tenant application.** UZE/UZED.si demand side first; affiliate
tenants get on-chain referral settlement without running infrastructure.

**Proof / metric.** Transaction success rate, median confirmation time,
cost per settlement (devnet until the Nov 10 gate).

**For the expert.** How much chain should the consumer ever see? Where
does "on-chain" become a selling point vs. a friction point?

## Skill 4 — cairo-starknet

**Strategic purpose.** Privacy as a product feature: shielded payments,
unlinkable identities, ZK-adjacent design — aligned with the UZD track
and the Starknet Shadow Accounts lineage.

**USP contribution.** "Private by architecture, not by promise." In a
market of surveillance-priced convenience, cryptographic privacy is a
premium differentiator — especially for high-value affiliate payouts.

**CTA mechanics.** "Pay privately" / shielded checkout as the premium
CTA tier above standard settlement.

**Tenant application.** UZD payment-tag track first; affiliate treasury
privacy second.

**Proof / metric.** Testnet privacy-settlement success; written threat
models per deployment (never marketing copy).

**For the expert.** Is privacy a headline USP or a quiet premium tier?
Which buyer persona pays for it?

## Skill 5 — genai-video-pipeline

**Strategic purpose.** Studio-grade video at agent speed and cost:
45s–3min short films, 10–40s UGC CTAs, platform-cut for Instagram and
TikTok. Replaces the outsourced video path entirely.

**USP contribution.** "A film studio that ships daily." The pipeline
(n8n → stills → motion → lip-sync QA → Remotion composite) produces
measured, beat-synced output — not prompt-and-pray clips.

**CTA mechanics.** The 10–40s UGC CTA is the conversion unit: every
film ends in a tracked action (profile creation, referral, ticket).
Video is not brand decoration; it is the top of the funnel.

**Tenant application.** 456iP brands first (L2Launch stories);
affiliates buy video as a managed-service line item.

**Proof / metric.** Cost per finished minute vs. outsourced baseline;
CTA click-through per cut; lip-sync QA pass rate.

**For the expert.** Which format earns the click — story or direct
CTA? What's the optimal CTA placement inside 10–40 seconds?

## Skill 6 — socialfi-feed

**Strategic purpose.** The lowest-friction affiliate onboarding in the
industry: 20-second profile creation that mints a real identity
(handle, blog, referral code, on-chain address).

**USP contribution.** "Twenty seconds to your own media business."
The profile *is* the product — every new user becomes a distribution
node with a QR share card.

**CTA mechanics.** The loop is the CTA: create profile → get QR card →
share → referrals → UZD earned → status. Verified activity (events
attended, not trades) is the social proof that fuels it.

**Tenant application.** UZE demand side first; every affiliate tenant
inherits the loop for their own audience.

**Proof / metric.** Profile-completion rate, K-factor (referrals per
user), time-to-first-referral.

**For the expert.** What single incentive maximizes the share action?
How is status best displayed to drive aspiration?

## Skill 7 — dashboard-builder

**Strategic purpose.** One pane for money in motion: treasury,
payouts, risk, messaging, and ad spend — Meta Business, Stripe Tempo,
Altitude.xyz, Range.

**USP contribution.** "See everything, approve anything." For
operators, the dashboard is command and control; for clients, it's
transparency as a trust product.

**CTA mechanics.** Operator CTAs: approve payout, screen address,
top up spend. Every financial CTA carries the per-action approval
discipline.

**Tenant application.** 456iP treasury first; affiliate tenants get
scoped views of their own money.

**Proof / metric.** Time-to-approve per action; reconciliation accuracy.

**For the expert.** Which three numbers does each persona check daily?
What's the one action the dashboard must make effortless?

## Skill 8 — linkedin-talent-editor (Vantage)

**Strategic purpose.** LinkedIn-grade professional authority at
influencer scale — the priority-1 voice. Two registers (professional,
influencer), one verified evidence base.

**USP contribution.** "Authority you can check." Every post traces to
a verified brief; the voice never invents expertise. In a feed of AI
slop, traceability is the brand.

**CTA mechanics.** LinkedIn-native: follow, DM, event signup, grant
application. Professional register converts to partnerships;
influencer register converts to distribution.

**Tenant application.** Founders and corporate handles first
(456iP, L2Launch, UZE); affiliate executives as a managed-service
offer later.

**Proof / metric.** Engagement rate per register; DM-to-conversation
rate; follower quality (not just count).

**For the expert.** Which register should lead for each brand? What's
the highest-converting LinkedIn CTA in this market?

## Skill 9 — podcast-producer (Airwave)

**Strategic purpose.** The L2Launch Podcast: builder stories from the
incubator, engineered as a client and affiliate magnet. YouTube now,
Spotify graduation over time.

**USP contribution.** "The builders are the marketing." Real founders,
real breakage, real outcomes — the show is proof of the incubator's
value, not a claim about it.

**CTA mechanics.** Subscribe; apply for grants; become an affiliate.
Every episode ends with one tracked action, and cutdowns feed the
short-form funnel.

**Tenant application.** L2Launch flagship; format licensed to
affiliate tenants as a managed-service line.

**Proof / metric.** Subscriber growth, episode-to-application rate,
cutdown CTR.

**For the expert.** What's the single CTA per episode? When is the
right moment to graduate to Spotify?

## Skill 10 — partnerships-voices (Bridge / Envoy / Harbor / Apex)

**Strategic purpose.** Four distinctly named, distinctly voiced BizDev
personas — never the same persona, never cross-posting lanes.

**USP contribution.** "Four voices, zero bleed." Corporate partners
hear exactly the voice they expect: the incubator's connector, UZE's
officer, UZED's officer, the VC specialist. Persona discipline *is*
the professionalism.

**CTA mechanics.** Partnership inquiries per lane: builders → Bridge,
venues/brands → Envoy, resale partners → Harbor, capital → Apex.
Each lane's CTA routes to the right relationship.

**Tenant application.** Corporate handles first; the persona system
itself becomes a managed-service template for affiliate brands.

**Proof / metric.** Inquiry rate per lane; partnership pipeline value;
zero lane violations.

**For the expert.** Does each persona need its own CTA, or one shared
partnership CTA with lane routing behind it?
