# Trading De-Emphasis and Inclusive Rails — Doctrine
**Dated directive of Hammond James Dugan IV — 2026-11-20.**
Filed in UZEmarkets and in Learn2Launch. This doctrine governs all
diagrams, planning documents, and outward framing from this date.

## 1. Trading is de-emphasized — everywhere
Digital asset trading, and trading in general, is **de-emphasized
through all of our Mermaid diagrams and all of our planning
diagrams**. Trading is an offering to **entity-based affiliates** —
it is not something for individuals. Outside of Learn2Launch and
its financial education track for youth, trading is **transparent
but not promoted**: not pushed to entity owners, not pushed to
youth, not placed in front of people.

## 2. What is promoted instead: the free and accessible treasury
What we promote is the availability of a **free and accessible
treasury for all applicants** — inclusive access to USD:
- **CASH** (Tempo)
- **USDC on Solana** and **USDC on Base**
- **USDG across Robinhood Chain and Ink**
Inclusive — and still not pushed in front of people. Access is the
offer; nobody is sold a trade.

## 3. Why — the regulatory and moral standpoint
Outside of our own token, token trading is **massively dangerous**.
Tokens are often trash at their core valuation, or total fraudulent
scams. The founder's own record, stated this date: his personal
token holdings since mid-September 2026 have dropped **between 50
and 90 percent**.
The exception is our own: **UZD**, our utility — the white-hat
token. White Hats are rewarded in **USDC stablecoin**; activity can
be rewarded in UZD, and we will discover how to use UZD. No other
token is promoted, endorsed, or placed in front of anyone.

## 4. Learn2Launch's frame
Learn2Launch is for **youth-through-adult learning and education** —
exposure through the **TradFi platforms** — and, most importantly,
for **organizational acceleration and access to inclusive rails
through blockchain linkage to traditional finance**. The rails we
promote run through **NEAR intents** and programs like **Base
Builder**, and our affiliations with **Robinhood Chain, Ink Chain,
and NEAR, as well as Solana**.
(Education care, standing: for youth under 18, the financial
education track is paper trading only — nothing beyond it is
encouraged.)

## 5. Gas, swaps, and donations (added same date, ~10:29 ET)
- **Gas: we cover our gas.** On the alignment between UZE and
  Decensat.org — the performant art and sport amateurs' resources —
  the periodic **programmatic donations cover our gas costs, and we
  make no spread on them**.
- **Swaps:** where someone swaps from a debit card or a bank into
  stablecoin, we make a **minor spread** — the additive-service case,
  nothing major.
- **Donations are segregated and separate.** Donations to the
  organization are never profit and never spread; they are held
  apart, for our operations and for maintaining the repositories.
