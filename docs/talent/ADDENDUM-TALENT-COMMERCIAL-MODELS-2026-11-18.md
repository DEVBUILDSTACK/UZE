# Addendum — Talent Commercial Models (James, 2026-11-18)

Filed as an addendum to `CONSOLIDATED-SCOPE-v3.1.md`. This addendum
fixes the commercial shape of external talent beyond the developer
vertical. It changes no phase, gate, or invariant of the scope of
record; it names the money models the marketplace layer (P3/P4)
must carry.

## Domains beyond developers

The Dev Library is the developer instance of a general pattern: a
prospect in any talent domain is served through the same agentic
Talent desk (desk@uzemarkets.com), with a customized portal on its
own subdomain per domain.

## Four engagement modes (external talent)

1. **On-site**
2. **Hybrid**
3. **Remote**
4. **Full outsourced contractor**

These map to the scope's location modes (ONSITE / HYBRID / REMOTE)
and its OUTSTAFF engagement type.

## Two money models — distinct, never blended

**Outstaffing.** UZE Markets pays the talent; UZE Markets bills the
client. The talent's paymaster is UZE.

**Placement.** UZE Markets places the individual for a fee of **at
least one month (30 days) of salary**; the engaging client — the
corporation that employs the individual for its use case — pays the
individual **directly**. The talent's paymaster is the client.

The four engagement modes apply under both models. Which model a
given engagement runs under is a term of the engagement itself,
recorded before work begins.

## Rail note

Where UZE Markets is the paymaster (outstaffing) or collects the
placement fee, settlement follows the standing rail preference law
(USDC on Solana by default; see the settlement matrix of record).
