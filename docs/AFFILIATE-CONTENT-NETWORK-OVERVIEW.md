that fills the venues, the deal flow that feeds the venture, and the

## The multi-agent engine: weekly/monthly, per vertical

Content runs on a **weekly/monthly cadence**, organized by vertical, each
with its own topical subject-matter expertise — the goal is genuine
influence in each vertical, not generic filler:

- **Hospitality** — stay, venues, travel intelligence
- **Major venue music concerts** — artists, tours, ticketing
- **Global athletic competitions** — LA2028 Olympics (incl. surfing,
  lacrosse), FIFA World Cup, F1 Grand Prix
- **VIP experiences** — VIP areas, boxes, special events, pre-event
  day offerings
- **Healthcare medical tourism** — patient stay, partner facilities

Each vertical gets dedicated agents with **distinct voices** — the written
track (n8n pipelines: scrape → curate → draft → publish, per-affiliate
tone config) and the motion track (Content Creator pipeline:
stills → AI video → lip-sync QA → beat-synced composite). Premium sources
are paid **per-article via x402** micropayments — pure usage, no
subscriptions. The KVM2 voice roster (GIRA, SoSim, SoSim Sisters, …)
extends the same voices into concierge and audio surfaces.

Cadence: **weekly** topical pieces per vertical (news, previews, guides),
**monthly** flagship expertise pieces (deep dives, rankings, market
intelligence). Every piece carries the affiliate's UZE code and
attribution — influence that pays.

## The affiliate multi-tenant blog network

Every affiliate business gets a branded home: **`{business}.uze.fi`** —
multi-tenant, one platform, per-affiliate branding, theme tokens on the
shared design system. Voice-built in the C3 console.

**The attribution loop:** affiliates share links across their social
outlets (blog, LinkedIn, X, Instagram) and every link points back to the
source — their uze.fi subdomain — which carries their UZE referral code.
Social reach converts into tracked referrals, which convert into UZD
earnings settled through the Web3 treasury. The blog is not a brochure;
it is the top of their funnel.

**The paid upgrade — domain masking:** affiliates can upgrade to mask the
subdomain: point their own domain at their blog (custom domain mapping +
redirect), fully white-labeled, no uze.fi visible. This is a revenue line
for the platform — vanity infrastructure sold to the affiliates who
outgrow the subdomain — while the attribution and settlement rails stay
underneath regardless of the domain on top.

## The growth suite is the activation arm

The multi-tenant offering is not just publishing — it is the full
**content innovation offering**: the blog network *plus* the growth
suite, tenant-scoped to each affiliate business. See
`GROWTH-SUITE-OVERVIEW.md`.

What the affiliate business gets, in one offering:

- Their branded `{business}.uze.fi` (or masked custom domain)
- Weekly/monthly vertical content in their voice
- Client-configured services: site analysis, SEO + GEO, prospecting,
  market scraping with list sequencing
- Cold outreach (Resend) + warm sequences (WhatsApp) run under their
  brand and offers
- Autonomous voice prospecting and support, with AI-to-human transfer
- Paid ads (Meta/FB/IG/Google) managed against the same offers
- The gamified SocialFi registration flow converting their audience
  into tracked referrals → UZD earnings

One tenant, one brand, one funnel — content, outreach, and conversion
under the same roof, settled through the same treasury.

## How it all connects

1. **Learn2Launch** sources and funds creators (grants, Quotrons/Ink
   track, builder program).
2. The **multi-agent engine** publishes weekly/monthly vertical expertise
   under each affiliate's voice and brand.
3. Affiliates distribute across socials; links resolve back to
   `{business}.uze.fi` (or their masked domain) with referral codes.
4. Conversions settle through the treasury → **UZD payouts**; the
   **monolithic backend** keeps the books (beneficiaries, LP records,
   yield).
5. **Fundio.vc** underwrites the next cycle — the flywheel: more
   affiliates sharing UZE codes → more events buying distribution →
   more affiliate earnings in UZD.
