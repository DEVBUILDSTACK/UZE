Where verification comes from — the validation channels

Data-layer evidence is submitted through two channels: email, and the NEAR Gateway's encrypted chat. Email is channel #1 today. We always prompt for the encrypted chat first — it is the channel being moved to #1 as adoption grows over the coming years, with email settling to #2.

#1 today — EmailSupporting documents arrive by email; identity is proven without exposing the address. A document carries no assumption of truth: its weight comes from the source's standing and the document's own verifiability, read by OCR where needed.
#1 in the making — Encrypted chatThe Gateway's E2EE v3 chat, attested inside a TEE — the Class B session below is this channel at work. Voice and chat together, phonetic transcription tied to the written language with the correct spelling held against the spoken word, are the adoption path; the Grok Voice plane is the working example.

What enters is both new and factual, and verifiably trusted; contribution is credited by how new and how factually verifiable it is. The position: replace media with factual, verified information that did not exist before — truth instead of propaganda or influencing.

DEALFi email verification — the evidence pools

A component of the DEALFi surface in our dashboard, part of the DFI organizational build. The pool pattern, in four steps:

01 · Create poolA buyer opens a pool naming the evidence needed and sets the budget.
02 · Submit proofA contributor submits a zero-knowledge proof of the evidence — the underlying data is never exposed.
03 · Verify on-chainThe proof validates on-chain without revealing the actual data.
04 · CreditedThe contributor is credited for usefulness — counted, never a payment in itself; standing is earned by milestones, agentic and human alike.
