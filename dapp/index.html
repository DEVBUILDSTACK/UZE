DEALFi Underwriting

Solutions

Valuation, extended. UZE Underwriting began with the P&L of hospitality and income properties. Under DEALFi it reads any income-generating operating company or project — the business and the real estate under it — on three planes: financials, valuation, and risk. DFI builds the marketplace technology; lending and brokerage belong to affiliates.
