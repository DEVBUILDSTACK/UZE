UZE Dev Partner FAQ

For development partners: how this page — the UZE Dev Library — works across all of its samples, from sign-in and browsing to the desks this page runs. Open a topic.

Access & sign-in

How do I sign in?
Enter the email address your access was granted to, choose "Email me a code", and enter the 6-digit code that arrives. Codes are one-time and live for 15 minutes. There is no password to keep.

I requested a code and nothing arrived. What went wrong?
The address must match the granted address exactly, character for character. A similar-looking address — a different mailbox at the same domain — is not on the list, and the page stays silent rather than say so: no code is issued and nothing is sent. Use the exact address on your invitation from the desk, and check your spam folder.

My code did not check out, or it expired.
Request a fresh code and use the newest one — asking for a new code replaces the one before it, and each code works once, within 15 minutes of being sent.

Browsing the catalog

How do I narrow the list?
Four filters sit above the catalog: Topic, Date, Repository, and Track. Each one narrows the samples shown; they combine, and setting one back to "all" clears that filter.

Why can I not see a sample a colleague can see?
What you see is set by your track and by any per-sample clearance on your record. Internal-only samples never appear in placement-track views, and the same holds in reverse.

The samples

What is a sample?
A plain-language description and a working mock of something built across DEVBUILDSTACK. The Library holds descriptions and mocks only — never source.

Where does the source live?
On GitHub, behind its own gate. Library access and source access are two separate grants, and source is granted per repository. If a source link answers 404, that is the gate working: you do not hold that repository yet.

A sample's name changed. Where did it go?
Names sync from the build record. When a sample is renamed, its old name is kept as an alias, and the old name still finds it.

What do the version and the changelog mean?
Every card carries a version number and a one-line changelog. When a sample changes, the version rises and the changelog gains a line saying what changed.

Commenting & suggesting

Can I comment before I hold any GitHub access?
Yes. Open any sample and use the Comments & suggestions box. Commenting is open from your first sign-in — no GitHub access needed.

What happens to my suggestion?
It is recorded on the sample, and the build lead reviews every one.

Tracks & expertise

What are Track A and Track B?
Every developer record on this page sits on one of two tracks. Track A is the internal track: developers who build for UZE Markets itself, and who are never placed with clients. Track B is the placement track: developers the desk can staff into client engagements.

What is a Role × Realm pair?
Expertise recorded as a cross. The role is what you do — smart-contract developer, data scientist — and the realm is the chain or field it lives in — Lightning, NEAR AI, cross-chain. A record holds up to five pairs, ranked from primary down.

Engagements & the money models

How does a placement happen?
The desk staffs every engagement. A client describes the role needed; the desk decides who fits and records the staffing.

What does a client see about me?
Skills only — your Role × Realm pairs. Your name and contact details stay hidden until both of two facts are recorded: a signed engagement and a deposit. Only then are you revealed to that client — never before both.

What are the two money models?
The engagement desk on this page works in two models. Outstaffing: UZE Markets pays the talent and bills the client. Placement: the desk places the talent for a fee of at least one month's salary, and the client pays the talent directly.

Assessments (Scope 2)

What is an assessment?
When an employer configures a role at the assessment desk on this page, an assessment is generated for that role from a curated template, filled to match the role's skills and seniority.

Who approves an assessment before it runs?
Two human gates, in order. Gate 1: the employer confirms the intent. Gate 2: the approver of record confirms the assessment is valid for the role. Nothing runs before both have signed, and an agent cannot sign either gate.

What is published about an outcome?
A redacted copy, under a pseudonym scoped to the engagement. No names, no emails, no company names — ever.

Access changes & resets

What happens when something about my access changes?
Changes to content and naming sync silently. A change that affects your access ends your session on the spot; you sign in again with a fresh code.

What does a reset do?
A reset removes all access — the Library and every GitHub source grant. The record of your history is preserved. Access begins again only if the desk grants it anew.

The two addresses — the UZE Dev Library & UZE Buildr Team

There are two addresses. Which is which?
This page — dev.uzemarkets.com — is the UZE Dev Library: descriptions and mocks of what has been built, for development partners. buildr-eco.uzemarkets.com is the UZE Buildr Team: the ecosystem room for launch principals and contributors — the verbal overview, the three diagrams, and the two design-surface documents, Human and Agentic, with their pillars. It is not a developer surface: no GitHub account, no password.

How do I sign in at buildr-eco.uzemarkets.com?
Open the address, type the email your access was granted to into the "Email address" field, and choose "Email me a sign-in code". A 6-digit code arrives at that address; type it into the "One-time code" field and choose "Sign in". Your home opens. Each address keeps its own code — a code for one does not open the other.

What do I see once I am in?
Your home opens on your name and your sections. The Ecosystem level is open to every contributor. Below it, the organization sections — UZE, L2 Launchpad, DealFi, Fundio — appear as pills along the top, and only the ones your role carries: a section that is not yours is simply not there to open.

I hold both surfaces. Which do I open first?
The ecosystem first — the whole picture — then the UZE Dev Library, where the work itself lives. On your Buildr Team home, the "Your other surface" band carries the button "Continue to the UZE Dev Partner Library →"; it brings you here, and this page asks for its own code.

Contact — the desk

Who do I contact, and how?
The desk: desk@uzemarkets.com. All communication about this page, its samples, and its engagements runs through the desk — questions, anything about an engagement, and suggestions that do not belong on a sample.
