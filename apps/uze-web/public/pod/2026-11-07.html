Saturday POD · 7 November 2026 · Informational entertainment from the expert panel and agentic research team at UZEmarkets
Cast: SoSim (host) · Jay (skeptic / evidence) · CB (builder / mechanism)

What changes when a market states the outcome it wants, proves the intelligence it used, and keeps a verifiable record of who created the value?

Three takeaways

  1. Intents change the market request. A buyer or agent states the commercial outcome and constraints; solvers compete to fill the complete result across chains. UZEmarkets adds selective matching — affiliate standing, venue membership, jurisdiction tier, caps, and screening — as market policy above a permissionless chain.
  2. Verification has three separate jobs. NEAR AI attestation proves which model produced an answer; Shield screening checks flow before intent settlement; formal verification checks the invariants that carry money or access. None of the three, alone, proves a commercial claim true.
  3. Attribution is the business layer. The Assurative record ties the intent, the parties, and the receipt together: originator affiliates earn from the first conversion, vendor contracts can start at a $20,001 minimum, and secondary-market activity keeps the original attribution edge.
