Join the project · Performant Pathways

Performant Pathways — the amateurs in art and sport

Hey — join the project. Said plainly: this is for performant amateurs. Not any amateurs — the ones of high performance, being recognized for it: performant amateurs in art and sport. Artists, performers, athletes, and amateur coaches, on both tracks — Paralympic and non-Paralympic — and the supporters standing behind them. Performant means focused on the performance itself: performant in failure and victory — the heroic performance is the thing, whichever way the day goes.

The pathway is carried three ways, all of them programmatic by design:

Programmatic fundingProgrammatic resource fundingCharitable programmatic funding

Funding, resources, and charitable support — assembled programmatically, aimed at one destination: the passionate and performant amateur becoming a sustainably employed, contracted professional, adding to the cultural value of their country and the world.

Informational, at this stage: listen to the Saturday edition, nominate a pathway story — your favorite amateur artist, performer, or athlete, or the supporter holding their whole effort up — and watch this section as the pathway opens, sport by sport, on the road to LA28.
